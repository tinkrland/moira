## ludwig (2024)

- fantasy: puzzle (the puzzles are the point; the show is built
  around deciphering them) + competence (secondary: a quiet man
  repeatedly out-thinks a police force)
- authority_stance: outsider-underestimated (a civilian passing as a
  detective chief inspector, constantly underestimated by the actual
  police around him)
- lead_flaw_treatment: flaw-tolerated (john's reclusiveness and
  social difficulty are treated with warmth, never as a problem to
  fix)
- romantic_arc: none (the failed marriage is backstory; no romantic
  through-line drives the lead)
- romance_dynamic: skipped (arc is none)

- lens: consulting-detective (a civilian puzzle-setter
  impersonating a detective chief inspector; no rank of his own)
- setting_scale: urban (cambridge: a city with a city department)
- mystery_mechanism: whodunit (assembling the who from
  coded puzzles across the run)

  family's calling)

- lead_flaw_treatment: flaw-tolerated (danny's hothead excesses are

- romantic_arc: none (the leads' romances are settled or peripheral;
  no romantic through-line drives the series)
- romance_dynamic: skipped (arc is none)

- lens: cop (three generations of nypd)
- setting_scale: metropolis (new york is a character)
- mystery_mechanism: none (the spine is the job and
