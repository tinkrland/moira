# about moira

moira is a personal context engine. it exists because you are one person
with many selves, and every system you use flattens those selves into one
profile - then mines the blend.

## the one-liner

one place where your context lives, split into personas you control,
shared only through explicit consent.

## the belief underneath

- **your context is yours.** what you study, build, read, love, and say is
  not engagement data. it is the raw material of you, and it deserves the
  same protection as your money.
- **separation is honest; blending is not.** the student, the founder, the
  character, the alter ego all exist. pretending they are one profile is
  the lie every "personal" product tells today.
- **sharing should be like lending, not like handing over.** you give a
  pass to one shelf of one persona for one purpose for a while - and every
  use is on the record.
- **deletion must be real.** deleting a persona in moira shreds its keys.
  the data becomes undecryptable, not hidden behind a flag.

## where the name comes from

personas as fates you choose to wear - and moira as the one place the
threads are kept.

## what it is not

- not another social network. nothing in moira is public by default.
- not an agent framework. moira is the memory and consent layer that
  agents and apps plug into.
- not a chatbot. the backend ships no chat surface at all; the cli, the
  api, and the browser surface are the interfaces.
