# rubric: setting_scale (layer 1, single)

the community scale where the story lives. classification, not
experience: it filters and labels, and it feeds compound affinities
("give me small-town whodunits"). the experiential consequences
(recurring community cast, everyone knows everyone) are recorded as
evidence, or as motifs, not as extra values.

## values

### rural
- definition: countryside, farmland, spread-out jurisdiction.
- counterexample: a village center where the whole cast walks to each
  other's houses. that is small-town.

### small-town
- definition: a small community where the cast recurs: the cafe, the
  pub, the mayor, the neighbors. stars hollow, death in paradise,
  easttown.
- how to observe: location shooting aside, the town itself has a
  recurring named cast.
- counterexample: a city neighborhood that functions as a small town.
  tag what the show gives you: if the community is the cast, it is
  small-town.

### suburban
- definition: commuter belt, residential sprawl around a city.

### urban
- definition: a city, but the city is not the story's spine.

### metropolis
- definition: a dense major city that functions as a character: new
  york, london, los angeles.
- how to observe: the scale of the city shapes the cases and the
  texture (precincts, boroughs, sprawl).
