# rubric: gift_origin (layer 2, single, core)

where the lead's special edge comes from, if there is one. the owner's
axis, born from a real distinction: psych is both nature and nurture
(shawn was born with eidetic recall and his father drilled him),
high potential is pure nature (morgan's iq 160, no training), and
patrick jane, who is literally named "the mentalist", has no innate
endowment at all: his edge is learned craft from his fake-psychic
years, pure nurture.

this separates the "quirky genius solves crimes" cluster that genre
labels flatten: shawn (both), morgan (nature), patrick (nurture) are
three different flavors of the same surface trait.

## how to tag

- ask: strip away training and practice. what is left?
- a learned skill with no innate endowment behind it is nurture.
- an inborn endowment the lead never trained for is nature.
- an inborn endowment that was then deliberately trained is both.
- an ordinary competent professional is none. none is a real value,
  not a gap: the rookie and blue bloods are none.

## values

### none
- definition: no special edge. competence built the normal way.
- examples: the rookie, blue bloods, ncis, mare of easttown.
- counterexample: a lead whose brilliance is treated by the show as
  exceptional even if never explained. that is nature, and the
  absence of explanation is not the absence of a gift.

### nature
- definition: born with it, untrained.
- owner examples: high potential (iq 160), unforgettable (carrie's
  hyperthymesia).
- counterexample: monk. his observation is innate but inseparable from
  his condition; still nature, but record the inseparability in the
  evidence, not the value.

### nurture
- definition: built. learned craft, trained method, practiced skill,
  no innate endowment behind it.
- owner examples: patrick jane in the mentalist (learned mentalism
  from being a fake psychic), neal caffrey in white collar (forgery
  and cons as learned craft).
- counterexample: sherlock in elementary, whose method is taught to
  others but whose processing speed is innate. when both exist, tag
  both.

### both
- definition: an innate endowment deliberately trained into mastery.

### acquired
- definition: the edge arrived later, from an event: infection,
  experiment, artifact, accident. not birth, not training.
- evidence exemplar: izombie. the visions came with the zombie
  infection; liv before the boat party had none of this.
- counterexample: a latent gift that an event merely unlocked. if the
  story frames it as always having been there, dormant, that is
  nature; the evidence line should say which the story claims.
- owner example: shawn spencer in psych, born with eidetic recall and
  drilled through childhood by henry.
- counterexample: a naturally gifted lead who wastes the gift. if the
  story never engages the training, it is nature, and the evidence
  notes the wasted potential.
