# rubric: gift_cover (layer 2, single, optional)

tagged only when the lead has a gift and must manage how it reads to
others. the masquerade is a taste signal: some viewers are here for
the hidden-gift dance, others find it exhausting. two directions of
disguise, and they are mirrors.

## how to tag

- tag gift_cover only when a gift exists (gift_origin other than
  none) and the world would react to it.
- ask: does the protagonist explain the gift as something it is not?
- if the gift is used openly, or there is nothing to hide, tag none.
- the cover is judged from inside the story, not the marketing.

## values

### none
- definition: no masquerade; the gift is what it appears to be, or
  nothing needs hiding.
- counterexample: a genius whose brilliance intimidates people. that
  is a reaction, not a disguise.

### skill-as-supernatural
- definition: a real learned or innate skill deliberately presented as
  magic, psychic power, or the supernatural. the cover is a lie the
  protagonist tells on purpose.
- how to observe: the gift works without the mask; the mask is for
  the audience inside the story.
- evidence exemplar: psych. hyper-observation and eidetic recall sold
  to the police as psychic visions. remove the word "psychic" and
  every case still solves.
- counterexample: a character who genuinely believes their own
  psychic story. that is a delusion or a con on themselves, not a
  managed cover; note the difference in evidence.

### supernatural-as-mundane
- definition: a genuinely supernatural gift explained away after the
  fact as forensic insight, hunches, science, or cold reading. the
  cover is post hoc: the truth works, then gets a plausible coat.
- how to observe: the visions arrive unbidden; the protagonist
  retroactively clothes them in ordinary language for the room.
- evidence exemplar: izombie. brain-eating visions delivered to the
  detective as forensic hunches and psychic vibes that must be
  justified in morgue-speak. lucifer: desire-reading excused as
  clever psychological reads.
- counterexample: an openly psychic consultant in a world where
  psychics are accepted. nothing is being disguised; the world
  already has a slot for the gift.

## boundaries

- gift_cover is not the world axis. izombie is supernatural-overlay
  in world: the masquerade exists because the world does not accept
  what she is. psych is realist in world: nothing supernatural
  exists, so the lie is pure con. the same cover can sit in either
  world; tag both axes independently.
- gift_cover is not authority_stance. the cover is played on the
  gift, not on the badge (ludwig impersonates a brother, not a
  power: that is identity, not gift_cover).
