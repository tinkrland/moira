# rubric: gift_kind (layer 2, multi, optional)

the specific endowment, when there is one. only tagged when the lead
has one; gift_origin is the required core axis and carries the weight.
this axis is the fine grain: "gifted lead" is a taste; "eidetic memory
specifically" is a sharper one, and the audience who loves shawn's
eidetic recall has opinions about carrie's hyperthymesia.

## values

### eidetic-memory
- definition: near-photographic recall of seen material.
- owner examples: shawn spencer in psych.
- counterexample: a lead who simply never forgets a face. hyperthymesia
  is autobiographical, not eidetic.

### hyperthymesia
- definition: perfect autobiographical memory, remembering lived days.
- owner example: carrie in unforgettable.
- counterexample: sherlock's mind palace, which is stored deliberately.

### high-iq
- definition: general cognitive horsepower, stated or shown as raw
  processing speed or breadth.
- owner example: morgan gillory in high potential.
- counterexample: a deep specialist with one domain of mastery. that
  is procedural-craft on protagonist_gift, not high-iq here.

### hyper-observation
- definition: reads rooms, details and micro-expressions others cannot.
- examples: monk, elsbeth.
- counterexample: a lead trained to notice details (post, holmes). if
  it is trained, pair the tag with gift_origin: nurture.

### supernatural-gift
- definition: the edge is genuinely impossible: visions, mediumship,
  seeing the dead.
- counterexample: psych. shawn's psychic act is a lie; the endowment
  is eidetic memory and the con is trained. tag that, not this.

## how the two axes pair

| work | gift_kind | gift_origin |
|---|---|---|
| psych | eidetic-memory | both (nature and nurture) |
| high potential | high-iq | nature |
| unforgettable | hyperthymesia | nature |
| the mentalist | (untagged: no endowment) | nurture |
| white collar | (untagged: no endowment) | nurture |
| the rookie | (untagged) | none |

untagged here means "no endowment to name", never "we did not check".
gift_origin: none is the explicit marker of no special edge.
