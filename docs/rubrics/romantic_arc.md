# rubric: romantic_arc (layer 2, single)

the lead's romantic through-line and what shape it takes across the run.
degree nuance ("excruciatingly slow, eight seasons of will-they-wont-they")
is not a separate value: the arc value carries the shape, and the degree
lives in wrangled motifs (docs/motifs-wrangling.md).

## how to tag

- exactly one primary, no secondaries (single-select).
- tag the lead's romance, not any romance on the show. an ensemble
  couple is out of scope unless their arc drives the series.
- judge at the series level for v0 (unit: series). a limited series is
  one run; an ongoing show is tagged for its dominant pattern.
- if there is no romantic through-line for the lead, the value is none.
  that is a real, scorable value, not a gap.

## values

### none
- definition: no romantic through-line for the lead.
- how to observe: the lead's story never runs on a romance; partners
  may exist and exit without shaping the arc.
- counterexample: a lead whose romance is the season's emotional spine
  even if it ends badly. that is central-premise, not none.

### slow-burn
- definition: a sustained will-they-wont-they as a subplot across a
  long stretch of the run.
- how to observe: seasons of proximity, near-misses and interruptions
  before the pairing lands (or before the show ends); the delay is the
  point and the pleasure.
- boundary: slow-burn is a subplot engine. if the romance is the
  premise, that is central-premise.
- counterexample: a couple that forms within a season and stays
  together. that is early-resolved.

### on-off-cycle
- definition: the pair gets together, comes apart, repeats.
- how to observe: multiple break-up and reunion beats across the run;
  the cycle itself is the pattern.
- counterexample: one break-up followed by seasons of slow rebuild.
  that is slow-burn, not a cycle.

### early-resolved
- definition: the pairing happens early-to-mid run and the
  relationship then carries as an established fact.
- how to observe: the will-they-wont-they resolves well before the
  end; the show explores the relationship rather than the chase.
- counterexample: a couple established in season one that keeps
  breaking up. that is on-off-cycle.

### central-premise
- definition: the romance is the show, not the seasoning.
- how to observe: each season or volume's emotional core is the
  romance itself; remove it and the work stops being the work.
- counterexample: a romantic subplot that runs alongside the actual
  premise. that is slow-burn (or on-off-cycle), never this.
